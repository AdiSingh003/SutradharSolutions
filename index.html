Meet the Founder

Suresh S Dikshit

Facility Management & Engineering Professional

Suresh S Dikshit brings over four decades of engineering and operations expertise spanning the Indian Army, Reliance Industries, and Reliance Communications. Having risen through Engineer to GM-level roles, he has led the design and commissioning of large-scale electrical and facility systems — including 130,000+ sq.ft. of raised-floor data centre space — and conducted assignments at ABB Sweden, Wartsila France, and Bechtel UK.

/ Our services

Everything your business needs to grow.

Focused consulting services, delivered by people who have done the work.

01

Facility Management Consulting

End-to-end facility management advisory — from operational audits and maintenance strategies to multi-site infrastructure planning for industrial and commercial facilities.

02

Data Center / IDC Engineering

Design, engineering, and commissioning of data centre infrastructure including power systems, cooling, redundancy architecture, and 24×7 operational frameworks.

03

Electrical Systems Advisory

Specification, selection, inspection, and commissioning of large-scale electrical equipment — DG sets, transformers, HT/LT panels, STGs, and bespoke power distribution systems.

04

Energy Efficiency & PUE Optimisation

Benchmarking and improving Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) for data centres and process plants, with validated conceptual measures that deliver real energy savings.

05

Project Engineering & Commissioning

Independent project engineering for large capital projects — from petrochemical plants and telecom rollouts to corporate facilities — covering design, vendor management, and site commissioning.

06

Training & Enablement

Workshops and practical playbooks to build in-house engineering capability so your teams can own and sustain operations confidently after engagement ends.
